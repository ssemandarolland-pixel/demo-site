Discover Uganda — The Pearl of Africa

Nestled in the heart of East Africa, Uganda is a land of breathtaking landscapes, extraordinary wildlife, vibrant cultures, and warm hospitality.

From the mist-covered mountains of Bwindi to the powerful waters of Murchison Falls, Uganda offers unforgettable experiences for travellers seeking adventure, beauty, and authentic African culture.

Known as the Pearl of Africa, Uganda is a destination where nature, adventure, and cultural heritage come together to create truly remarkable experiences.

Where Is Uganda?

Uganda is a landlocked country in East Africa, known for its beautiful landscapes, diverse wildlife, and rich natural resources.

Location

Uganda lies in East Africa and shares borders with Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and South Sudan.

Capital City

Kampala is Uganda's capital and one of its major centres of business, culture, and daily life.

Lake Victoria

Uganda shares the shores of Lake Victoria, one of the largest freshwater lakes in the world.

Natural Beauty

From rolling hills and lush forests to savannahs, mountains, and rivers, Uganda offers remarkable natural diversity.
