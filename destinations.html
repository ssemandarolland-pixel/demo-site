Our destinations

Discover the beauty of Uganda, the Pearl of Africa. Explore beautiful ladscapes, wildlife, mountains, lakes, and cultural attractions.

1. Murchison Falls National Park

Visit the powerful Murchison Falls and enjoy wildlife viewing, boat rides and a beautiful scenery.

2. Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Discover mountain gorillas and enjoy the beauty of Uganda's tropical forests.

3. Queen Elizabeth National Park

Enjoy wildlife safaris and see elephants, lions, hippos and many bird species.

4. Lake Bunyonyi

Relax beside the beautiful lake, enjoy canoeing and admire the surrounding hills.

5. Jinja

Visit the source of River Nile and enjoy beautiful scenery and exciting outdoor activities
