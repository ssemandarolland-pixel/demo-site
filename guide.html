📅 Best Time to Visit

  • June - September & Dec - Feb: Dry seasons, best for gorilla trekking & safaris
  • March - May & Oct - Nov: Green season, fewer tourists, beautiful photos

Travel Tips

  • Visa: $50 online before arrival
  • Money: Ugandan Shillings + USD cash, MTN Mobile Money works everywhere
  • Transport: We provide 4x4 safari vans with driver-guide
  • Safety: Uganda is safe, people are very friendly - "You are welcome"
